*2026-03-01 12:00 -  21:48 PST*

A band of [[Camarilla]] including [[Calimastia]], [[Kurokage]], and [[Simon]] along with the [[Sheriff]], [[Barry]] and a smattering of neonates to raid the [[Sacramento History Museum]], the domain of the [[Anarchs|Anarch]]-aligned vampire [[Neres]].

[[Thalia]] managed to incapacitate [[Barry|Barry]], whose goons had failed to extract him from the premises, leaving him even worse off in a car crash.

Meanwhile, [[Simon]] and [[Kurokage]] broke into the museum's lower reaches. [[Kurokage]] attacked [[Neres]] to distract her while [[Simon]] stole three artifacts.

[[Calimastia]] and her friend(?), [[Irene]] battled with [[The Jackal]] and [[Thalia]], in which [[Calimastia]] ***barely*** managed to incapacitate [[Thalia]]. At this point in time, [[Simon]] rushed past, put [[Kaveh]] in a bubble, and ran into the streets pursued by [[Kingsnake]] as well as [[Calimastia]]. In the end, [[Simon]] managed to fly off thanks to [[Calimastia]] interposing herself between him and an incensed [[Kingsnake]]. After a hard fought battle, [[Kaveh]] managed to down [[Calimastia|Cali]]. Then both [[Kaveh]] and [[Kingsnake]] negotiated with [[Irene]], trading [[Calimastia]] and a good word with [[Neres]] for a minor boon.

At the same time, [[Frances]] arrived on the scene around that time to tend to the wounded. Unseen, [[Gabriela]] pulled [[Barry|Barry]] out of the flaming car and [[Thalia]], though she couldn't see [[Gabriela]], saw [[Barry|Barry]] and decided (perhaps against her better judgement) to make a change and choose peace. [[Frances]] rushed over to heal the man and, despite [[The Jackal]]'s offer to execute the [[Sheriff]], [[Thalia]] opted to stake [[Barry|Barry]] through the heart, locking him in torpor. [[Gabriela]] then broke obfuscate to pull two [[Ventrue]] goons from the car crash and left after a brief exchange with the [[Anarchs]].

[[Neres]] was less than thrilled with the performance of the [[Anarchs]], seeing as several of her artifacts were stolen. Though after some discussion, she considered allowing them another chance to prove themselves worthy of her support. [[Frances]] left with [[Barry|Barry]] in the back of his old Volkswagen to a safe location.

[[The Jackal]] and [[Thalia]] went to speak briefly, [[The Jackal]] expressing something akin to concern regarding a pair of oddly familiar faces in [[Irene]] and [[Calimastia]], the relics, and [[Thalia]]'s handle on the situation. She assured them the [[Anarchs]] would be rectifying the situation soon enough, then they parted ways.
