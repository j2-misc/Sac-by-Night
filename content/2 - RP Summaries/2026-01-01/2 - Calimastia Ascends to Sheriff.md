[[Calimastia]] did some bullshit I'm not allowed to publish, but the end result was that she was made [[Sheriff]].

%%
After being revived by her situationship, [[Calimastia]] returned to the [[Sterling Hotel]] to report to the [[Prince]] directly, offering [[Lauren]], the [[Keeper of Elysium]], a boon in exchange for the honor. She reported what she knew of the situation and, in a show of opportunistic ambition, righteous passion, some mysterious third thing, or a combination of all the above, [[Calimastia]] nominated herself as interim [[Sheriff]]. This seemed to immediately polarize the court (or most of it, as the [[Banu Haqim]] [[Primogen]], [[Kabir]], was not present).

[[Jacobi|Archmage Jacobi]], of course supported this, as did [[Amalia|Lady Amalia White]] and [[Reginald|Reginald King]]. [[The Talon]] was the first to voice proper dissent.

[[Mark]] was then quick to dismiss everyone from the room, save for [[Calimastia]]. He questioned her reasoning, her *real* reasoning, and what assurances he'd have she wouldn't betray him. [[Barry|Barry]], dullard though he was, was fiercely loyal. [[Calimastia]]'s answers focused primarily on her effectiveness as a warrior, and her disposability. This seemed to sway the [[Prince]] enough to make her interim [[Sheriff]], on the condition that if [[Barry|Barry]] was alive and captured, she was to rescue him.

The inauguration was long and tedious, but eventually unanimous. Save for, of course, the absent [[Banu Haqim]] [[Primogen]].
