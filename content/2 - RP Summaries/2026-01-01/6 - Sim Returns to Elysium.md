[[Simon]] returned to the [[Sterling Hotel]] after a semi-successful mission[^1], and reported what had happened.

> *Simon cannot help it. He cracks his neck. Loudly.* "I apologize for this." *He says and looks at all the people present.* "Earlier this evening, you sent us to carry an attack on [[Neres|Neres Zauvasi]]'s museum, to break the spirit of the anarchs and retrieve some relics in their possession. I regret to inform you that our [[Sheriff]][^2] has fallen, brought to torpor by a red haired [[Anarchs|Anarch]][^3]. They also have a sniper in their rank, they got me. And a rather large kindred wielding a hammer. A [[Nosferatu]] called [[Kurokage]] and myself managed to sneak into the museum, and just as we were about to enter, I saw [[Anoush Jindal|Anoush Jindal]] running away. I found a hidden room, and managed to retrieve these." *He says and pulls the necklace and the basket out of his pockets.* "Unfortunately, [[Kurokage]] stole the third artifact from me. It was a flint knife." *He adds, trying to keep his voice even, but some frustration can definitely be heard.*

He credited his escape largely to the efforts of [[Calimastia|Calimastia Ayton]]. Though, he needed to fly in order to do so as well, endangering the Masquerade. For his efforts, his punishment would be decided by [[Jacobi|Archmage Jacobi]] instead of [[Mark|Prince Sterner]]. The punishment (if you could even call it that) was to simply surrender the artifacts to the [[Jacobi|Archmage]].

After the meeting, [[Simon]] conversed in private with [[Lauren|Lauren Harris]], the [[Keeper of Elysium]]. She agreed he did what he had to but made a bad call, and suggested he pick up something to help with bullets so he wouldn't have to resort to flight. As for the [[Primogen|Primogen Council]], she lamented [[Jacobi]]'s recent obsession with "relics that speak of a bygone era." She believed he'd been hearing voices since [[Dorian|Prince Dorian]] met final death.

When asked, she said she didn't know anything about [[James]]'s current whereabouts.
