# Overview
The gala at [[Palazzo Giovanni]] was the server's first major event following the first day[^1]. A lot of players were there, a new one was introduced, and a lot of people did a lot of things all at once. It was very hard to keep track of! In order to keep things organized, this article breaks the usual RP summary format and will instead give a brief overview of everything that happened and link to related articles with more information if one is available.

For a genuinely pretty sloppy attempt at cataloging everything as it happened in order, with maximal detail, see [[1 - The Gala, Unabridged Chaos]].
# Grand Opening
- [[Barry]] Ragebait
Toward the beginning of the event, [[Thalia]] ragebaited [[Barry]] into throwing her across the floor and made a whole scene about it. [[Calimastia]] quickly went into damage control mode, no one was particularly happy, but in the end [[Thalia]], playing the victim, offered a boon to [[Barry]] who used it to organize a future duel

> *[[Barry]] jabs a finger at [[Thalia]].*
> "You and me will finish this, soon. No claws. That's the boon."

- [[The Jackal|Jackal]] Dance
[[The Jackal]] invited [[Calimastia]] to a dance which had some kind of undertones for sure. Unclear of what exactly, but something's going on there. [[Calimastia]] managed to recognize them.

> "I see a caitiff. A *pander*. Childer of [[Kellan]]. The destroyer."
> 
> *She says, with increasing intensity. Swifter tempo made for more focused footwork, but [[Calimastia]] was nothing if good at multitasking. She kept her eyes firmly on [[The Jackal|Jackal]].*
> 
> "You still bear that name? Bold. Foolish. Your designs have long been dashed."

- Grand Theft Vitae
[[Thalia]] assembled a crack team ([[Roy]], and [[Kiri|Kiri Sol]]) to secretly steal some of [[Amalia|Amalia White]]'s blood. They managed this successfully, and [[Kiri]] left shortly thereafter, having frenzied and left to go kick rocks.

> See [[4 - Grand Theft Vitae]]
# Meetings with Ernesto
[[Gabriela]] met with [[Francesca Pisanob|Francesca Pisanob]] and told her about what had happened the previous night[^2]. She was then directed to [[Fiora|Fiora Milliner]], who took her, [[Kaveh]], and [[Jensen]] to speak with [[Ernesto Giovanni]]. After informing him of the threat, which he dismissed as a problem for later[^3], [[Kaveh]] urged him to join the [[Anarchs]]. [[Gabriela]] insisted he instead remain neutral, and [[Ernesto Giovanni|Ernesto]] seemed more likely to follow [[Gabriela]]'s advice than [[Kaveh]]'s.

Then, [[Simon]] and [[Calimastia]] met with [[Ernesto Giovanni|Ernesto]], twisting his arm with the Promise of 1528 until he signed a contract for an exclusive deal with the [[Camarilla]] though technically maintaining the [[Giovanni]]'s position as an independent party. Just an incredibly partisan one.

> See [[3 - Old Grudges, Meetings]]
# The Rest of the Party
- [[The Jackal|Jackal]] stuff
[[The Jackal|Jackal]] met with a man named [[Toby]] and after a conversation about [[Ernesto Giovanni|Ernesto]]'s superstition regarding Gehenna, they went off to find some trouble. [[The Jackal|Jackal]] found said trouble in the form of two [[Ravnos]] goons trying to stir up some trouble with a poorly thought out plan. This eventually led them into a conversation with [[Vale]] which would later be cut off by the appearance of one [[Mazhar|Mazhar of Cappadocia]]

- [[Luke Grayson|Luke]] and [[Thalia]]
After coming back to the party, [[Thalia]] was chaperoned by [[Luke Grayson]], who offered to bribe her to fuck off to LA. She said no, and he asked her to meet with him at [[The Ziggurat]] to discuss it further, using Dominate to ensure her presence.

- [[Bernard|Father Bernard]] sighting
At some point during the evening, [[Jensen]] spotted his sire in the crowd, conspicuously just before the arrival of [[Mazhar]]. Perhaps the two events could be related.

- [[Calimastia]] and [[Irene]]
A personal 9/11, [[Irene]] ambushed [[Calimastia]] with a dance she couldn't deny, during which they spoke of a similar strange relationship between [[Marouth]] and one [[Gabriela|Miss Ortega]], the reappearance of [[The Jackal|Jackal]], and their plans for the future of the [[Tremere]] Chantry.
# Mazhar of Cappadocia
> *The Jackal disappears into a suddenly parting crowd. The Hound finishes briefing the Prince on her latest victory, her voice the last of a silenced crowd. The blacksmith takes the hand of the curator, only for her to look frightened at the door before their dance can begin.*
> 
> *The man with the clay colored toga steps through the front doors. His thin, curly hair is grey at the roots. His hands are discolored and emaciated. Across his face is a red mask, made of cheap plastic, and his black eyes reflect no light. The doors close on their own, behind him. He coughs and speaks up in a raspy voice:*
> 
> *"A gala. How lovely."*

[[Mazhar|Mazhar of Cappadocia]] appeared, declared that [[Augustus Giovanni|Augustus Giovanni]] had died, and that whoever helped him kill the most [[Giovanni]] would gain his help no strings attached. Chaos ensued. Here's the gist:

- [[Mazhar]] killed everyone blocking him on his way to [[Ernesto Giovanni|Ernesto]], who he killed shortly thereafter.
- [[Javier]] heard "no strings help for [[Giovanni]]cide" and thought it sounded like a great deal.
- [[Thalia]] tried to talk the bloodthirsty [[Anarchs]] down, and did so successfully, opening her up to an attack from [[The Talon]] who only backed off when [[Javier]] began attacking them.
- [[Gabriela]] grabbed a knife and interposed herself between [[Marouth]] and [[Francesca Pisanob|Francesca Pisanob]], eventually fleeing under cover of magical shadow.
- [[Kaveh]] did the same between a litany of unnamed, bloodthirsty goons and [[Fiora|Fiora Milliner]] who later helped him escape.
- [[Calimastia]] disarmed [[Irene]] and tried to attack [[Mazhar]] with the help of [[The Jackal|Jackal]] and [[Vale]] without much success.
- [[Vale]] and [[The Jackal|Jackal]] managed to grapple and trip [[Mazhar]] with Obtenebration and martial arts, respectively. [[Kiryu]] was also there but neither he nor [[Calimastia]]'s weapons were particularly effective against the Methuselah (nor those of anyone, for that matter).
- [[Mazhar]] sent out a wave of depression to his immediate attackers, incapacitating all but [[Vale]] which seemed to impress him. He allowed [[Vale]] to leave and even asked to speak with him sometime.

> For more specifics, see [[5 - The Gala, Unabridged Day 2]]. Lots of good writing. Worth a read.
