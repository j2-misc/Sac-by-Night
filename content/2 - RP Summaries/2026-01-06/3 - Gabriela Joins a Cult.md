*2026-03-06 15:50 - 18:40 PST*

[[Gabriela]] met with [[Reginald]] at [[The King Blood Bank]]. He lamented the recent attack on his [[Malkavian Cattle Ranches|Cattle]][^1] and the capture of one of his coteries, but they got to business quickly. They gathered in cold storage with other [[Malkavian|Malkavians]], dressed in cult robes, and communed with the childe of the founder of their clan.

This voice spoke only in Spanish, a language only [[Gabriela]] could fully understand of the kindred present. It said the following:

> **"For the mission. Pour the blood of the clans into the river. For the mission."**

> **"This woman speaks for me. This woman speaks for me."**
> *Gabriela suddenly feels the weight of a distant yet potent presence, settling upon her.*
> **"Help her draw the blood of the clans. Bring her the blood of the clans, to be poured into the river. For the mission."**

[[Gabriela]] relayed this to the rest of the Malkavians and told them in no uncertain terms that they needed to pour blood from the thirteen clans into the river to awaken [[The Saint]]. [[Reginald]] took them there, they both gave their blood, and in a hunger frenzy, [[Reginald]] fed on [[Madame Vogue]] who seemed to be human. A ghoul perhaps. [[Gabriela]] devoured the horses that drew their carriage, and after stabilizing [[Madame Vogue]], they walked back to the blood bank.

[[Gabriela]] left [[Reginald]] with her phone number, instructing him to call her if he received any donations. Otherwise, she'd be back when she had more blood[^2].
