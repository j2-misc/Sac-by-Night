After the previous night's scare[^1], [[Gabriela]] stayed the night with [[Zora]]. The following night, she received a text from [[Sam]] that she was going out to the bar. [[Zora]] then told [[Gabriela]] that [[Kabir]] wanted them to investigate a new potential cure for his condition.

[[Zora]] drove them in her run-down Pontiac to [[Kabir]]'s apartment. She asked what [[Gabriela]] had been busy with lately, and she informed her about some of her recent business with the [[Giovanni]], wanting to keep herself safe from [[Mazhar]], not trusting him to keep his word that he'd forgiven her from the previous night.

[[Kabir]]'s apartment was hardly more luxurious than [[Zora]]'s, but he excitedly invited them both in and told them they'd be meeting with a [[Doctor Prescott]], a doctor whom he'd met with nearly a century ago and was convinced could help heal him. [[Zora]] and [[Gabriela]] were both suspicious but hid this from [[Kabir]].

They ventured to a shady ass warehouse by a river where they met with [[Doctor Prescott]]. He looked sickly, and had an eye in the center of his forehead.
