[[Gabriela]] took [[Sam]] on another hunt. [[Sam]] insisted they go after a drug dealer, but [[Gabriela]] steered them toward a man convicted of several counts of aggravated assault. The reasons were obvious, but left unsaid. Once they got there, the target was easy enough to take down, but [[Sam]] was more interested in his wallet and the drugs in his jacket than his blood.

Before they could discuss anything, though, they heard their car alarm outside. [[Gabriela]] went to investigate and saw her car partly broken into and a man in a trench coat carrying away the [[Nosferatu]] she'd ran into the other night[^1]. She'd heard rumors about recent kindred killings, and hoped the culprit wasn't who she thought it was. Either way, she and [[Sam]] left to drop off their victim at the police station.

Notably, [[Mordecai]] wasn't there, so she asked [[Reyes]] to leave a message for her.

[[Sam]] and [[Gabriela]] returned home and after much questioning, [[Gabriela]] finally started talking about some of the things she was hiding.
