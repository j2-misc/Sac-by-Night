[[Calimastia]] went to feed at a bar, but as she left, she could sense someone watching her. That someone was none other than [[Mordecai|Lieutenant Mordecai Hayes]]. She noticed that he wasn't sweating, and she couldn't sense a heartbeat.

He asked how she sustained herself (and didn't seem to like the fact she maintained a herd). But otherwise proceeded with questioning her as to anything she knew about [[Lorenzo|Father Lorenzo]] or [[Bernard|Father Bernard]]. She knew neither, as she didn't tend to associate with [[Lasombra]]. Then, they parted. She suggested he see the [[Prince]] to be acknowledged as kindred, but he calmly stated he was responsible only to the city of Sacramento.

She then reported him to the [[Scourge]].
