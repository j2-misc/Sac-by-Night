*2026-03-15 13:12 - 20:16 PST*

[[Calimastia]] was called to a private meeting with [[Jacobi|Archmage Jacobi]] wherein he asked her to look for relics at [[The Cathedral of Blessed Sacrament]], and warned her there were traitors within the [[Tremere]][^1]. He told her of a thief who'd stolen one of his relics[^2], something she'd surely look into later.

At [[The Cathedral of Blessed Sacrament|The Blessed Sacrament]] attempted to listen for the voices of the relics. She was immediately intercepted by [[Frances|Father Frances]] who offered to help her in looking for the relics. They found a hatchet and a long wood pipe buried out back, listened to it whispering of [[The Saint]]. The relics had been long buried, but only began to whisper to people recently. Perhaps it was a sign of something.

[[Frances]] allowed her to take the relics, but would hold onto one until [[V&S]]'s excavation near the cathedral was dealt with. She'd talk to [[Jacobi]]. 

While he waited for her return, [[Frances]] examined the relic left with him, the pipe. It was no mere magic item, forged and buried, but a mundane object infused with power. The infusion, he believed was of one's soul. He spoke with the soul and it told him [[The Saint]] would return, that they burned her once by the river but she would return. Afterwards, he destroyed the pipe.

---

[[Calimastia]] returned to [[Jacobi]], hatchet in hand. She told him [[The Cathedral of Blessed Sacrament|The Cathedral]] was bare of all but one relic she'd retrieve for him, and that [[V&S]] had been seized by unknown forces and plotted against him. He set out to investigate, but she quickly suggested simply dissolving the company entirely. He was easily convinced. Then, she returned to [[Frances]] to inform him of what had happen.

For his part, [[Frances]] explained what he did with the relic in more polite words. He explained it possessed a tormented soul, and that he'd prayed and freed its soul. Though he did pray, and did something one could argue was freeing the soul, it wasn't thanks to the lord, but occult knowledge and Obtenebration. As an alternative offering to her [[Primogen]], he suggested investigating the river, which she left to do alone.

---

As [[Calimastia]] stalked the river, she heard a voice in Spanish. She couldn't fully understand it. "Blood," "river," "the mission." She tested the waters and found blood strangely permeating within it, belonging to the [[Banu Haqim]], [[Malkavian|Malkavians]], and [[Nosferatu]]. It was thirsty. It took her blood and despite her best efforts, the damage was done.

Then, she reported back to [[Jacobi]]. She told him about [[The Saint]] and their apparent presence within the river. She suggested he form a new excavation company posthaste to investigate further. Finally, she asked for some information, and a relic to present to the [[Giovanni]] at the upcoming gala.

---

Finally finally, [[Calimastia]] met with [[Don Valentin Cabrera|Don Valentin]], seeking out some of his finest craftsmanship to wear to the gala. He'd take a fine chainmail breastplate, apply the symbol of the [[Hound of the Sheriff]], and have it sent to her before the gala.
