*2026-03-07 11:40 - 18:25 PST; 2026-03-08 11:03 - 12:17 PST*

[[Gabriela]] and [[Simon]] met at [[Sacramento State University]] to discuss the plot to kill one [[Holly Zantosa]][^1]. They managed to sneak into a party at a theater through the side entrance, and confirmed the presence of private security detail. Inside, their mark was on stage. Her voice was enchanting; her presence, enthralling. Clearly she had some form of kindred powers. [[Simon]] would later determine, though, that she wasn't a kindred but instead a Revenant. A sort of selectively-bred ghoul made by some vampires over in Europe.

Thanks to [[Simon|Sim]] providing a distraction, [[Gabriela]] was able to sneak ahead backstage where she was caught despite unseen presence by [[Holly Zantosa|Holly]]'s manager, [[Fred Zantosa|Fred]]. They shared a familial resemblance. Unrelatedly, [[Fred Zantosa|Fred]] had a frankly insane twitter feed. Chaos ensued as the two would-be assassins tried to scramble away without getting into too much trouble, and in the end they made it out without too much issue. Though [[Gabriela]] noted a tall woman with a wicked scar, seemingly additional security.
