[[Kingsnake]], [[Thalia]], [[Kaveh]], and [[Jensen]] went to [[Mercury Labs]] and spoke to a thin-blood by the name of [[Dr. Mercury]]. They were informed that intruders had stolen two alchemical formulae. The only two surviving guards reported attacks from huge monsters, one resembling a horse with a snake head, something like a dragon, and a huge lion. [[Dr. Mercury|Mercury]] suggested looking into the illegal drug trade, as one of the recipes required a truckload of heroin; the other, a ton of medical grade adrenaline.

After some investigation work, and with the help of [[Kingsnake]]'s criminal connections, they got a location and a name: [[Elio]]. This led them to a warehouse (possibly part of a larger operation) and then to an abandoned laboratory called [[Kelvin Labs]], slated for demolition. There they met [[Elio]]. Apparently, his intent was to end the practice of Blood Bonding, a subject [[Kingsnake]] had never been made privy to. In the end, they reached an agreement with the [[Sabbat]] [[Elio]] to allow him to keep a copy of the research so he can continue his work, possibly with [[Anarchs|Anarch]] backing in the future.

[[Thalia]] was not particularly thrilled about this, as [[Elio]] was a member of the [[Sabbat]].
