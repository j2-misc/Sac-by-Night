[[Calimastia]] went to the [[Sterling Hotel]] to present the offer she'd received from [[Frances|Father Frances]][^1]. There she saw [[Kabir]] and [[The Talon]], seemingly arguing about something. [[Kabir]] seemed to hold some disdain for her, perhaps viewing her as a symptom of his larger disenfranchisement from the [[Primogen|Primogen Council]]. She offered [[Kabir]] a trivial boon for her failure to properly introduce herself at the start of the interaction and he said it'd be paid should she urged the [[Prince]] to consider his plans for retaking the [[Crocker Art|Crocker Art Museum]]. She assured him it'd be done.

Then, [[Luke Grayson]] emerged from the [[Prince]]'s court. She informed him of the plot to ambush him at [[Serena Bar]][^2], and declared she'd have the building under the protection of the [[Sheriff|Sheriff's Office]]. He thanked her for the offer, and went about his business to some meeting or other at [[The Ziggurat]].

Finally, [[Calimastia]] made her way in to see the [[Prince]]. He seemed in good spirits. Then she reported what she'd been up to since ascending to [[Sheriff]].

> "Your grace. I have several matters of varying import. I will proceed in order of events, and you may comment as you see fit along the way."
> 
> *She clears her throat, then speaks.*
> "Firstly, an attempted Anarch incursion into midtown has been repelled and half of its perpetrators have been turned over to an upstanding [[Camarilla]] member for an accounting on Monday[^3]. On Tuesday, I agreed and successfully assisted with [[Fiora|Fiora Milliner]]'s concern of a Masquerade breach in the suburbs, which has not only been dealt with, but I have also earned the ear of [[Ernesto Giovanni]] at an upcoming gala[^4]. Finally, yesterday, I contracted a more measured member of [[Anarchs|The Anarch Movement]] and negotiated for the return of [[Barry|Barry Brickhouse]][^5]. I present these terms to you for your approval or rejection at your sole discretion."
> *She presents the paper to the Prince with a bow.*

She believed [[Frances|Father Frances]]'s word was genuine, but doubted the [[Anarchs]] were completely devoid of schemes. To this end she suggested testing [[Barry]]'s blood for bounds once he was returned. [[Mark]] agreed to the terms regarding the creation of a new Elysia, but noted the following:

> "Before you go, some thoughts."
> *Mark glances back at the paper.*
> "I am not opposed to the creation of a second Elysia. I do not even mind the preservation of a neutral territory. But I am opposed to this entitled notion that we ought to pay for 'damages.' If [[Neres|Miss Zauvasi]] would like her museum to remain in good condition, she ought to reconsider her allegiance to the [[Anarchs]] who have, most likely, not lifted a finger to offer the reparations she seeks."
> "Such a notion is an offense to my dignity, and the dignity of [[Camarilla|The Ivory Tower]]."

> "I most agree your grace. But ultimately, the damages to the museum are a pittance compared to the boon we gain by the return of the [[Sheriff]]. Additionally, if we are to win this conflict without violation of the first tradition, we will need to show our adversaries the lengths the [[Camarilla]] can go to pay both its debts and its community."
> *She bows her head.*
> "If you wish to see that clause stricken I will see it done. But given [[Anarchs|Anarch]] failure to protect her artifacts, it would seem prudent to attempt to show goodwill to [[Neres|Miss Zauvasi]] that brings no harm to ourselves. Planting the seed of doubt may be a weapon rather than a conceit."

> *Mark sighs.*
> "This is what I miss about [[Barry]]. He would have had no objections. But fine, I'll pay the pittance for [[Neres|Miss Zauvasi]]'s own, self-inflicted grievances."

Then, she suggested the [[Anarchs]] were illegally siring fledglings to contribute to the war effort.

> *Mark scoffs.*
> "Is this what the [[Anarchs]] have come to? Siring fledglings en masse to flood the streets with petty soldiers? Kill them, and their disgraceful sires."

Finally, she paid off her trivial boon to [[Kabir]]. [[Mark]] would see to him next. When she informed him, [[Zora|Zora Tazri]] was with him.
