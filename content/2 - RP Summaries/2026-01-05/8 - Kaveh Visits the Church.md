*2026-03-05 21:09 - 23:37 PST*

[[Kaveh]] visited [[The Cathedral of Blessed Sacrament|The Cathedral]] to meet with [[Frances|Father Frances]]. They had some private business to discuss, and as there was no private place in the church, they decided to head to the [[Falcron Blacksmith]]. On the way, [[Kaveh]] asked how the church had been, and was surprised to learn that the [[Camarilla]] hadn't attacked since the events on New Year's[^1]. [[Frances]] asked of his childer and [[Kaveh]] explained that he'd sired one, [[Ember]], but the other, [[Jensen]], was taken in after being abandoned by his sire.

Once they arrived, [[Kaveh]] shared with the good father his encounter with [[Mordecai|Lieutenant Hayes]]. Specifically, he asked him about [[Lorenzo|Father Lorenzo]] and [[Bernard|Father Bernard]]. [[Frances]] could speak to [[Lorenzo]]'s character, and assured [[Kaveh]] he was a good man, but he'd yet to properly meet [[Bernard]]. [[Kaveh]] filled in the gaps, informing the father that [[Bernard]] had abandoned [[Jensen]] after siring him. [[Frances]] resolved to speak with both his brothers of this, especially [[Bernard]].

Finally, [[Kaveh]] asked [[Frances]] to speak with [[Jensen]], as he still struggled with being a vampire. [[Frances]] said to send him to [[The Cathedral of Blessed Sacrament|The Cathedral]] where he'd speak with him, though he'd ensure [[Bernard]] wasn't there, per [[Kaveh]]'s request. Then as he left, [[Kaveh]] presented his two pins. An eagle with a hammer; an eagle, a gavel. [[Frances]] took the one with the hammer, and was gone.
