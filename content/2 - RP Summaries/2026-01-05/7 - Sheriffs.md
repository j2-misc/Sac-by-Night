*2026-03-05 17:39 - 20:57 PST*

After picking him up from [[The Cathedral of Blessed Sacrament|The Cathedral]][^1], [[Calimastia]] brought [[Barry|Barry Brickhouse]] to a dark alley. She wrenched the stake from his chest.

> "...Sheriff."
> *She looks down at him.*
> "We have a lot to discuss."

> *[[Barry]], without much grace, picks himself up and slumps against the alley.*
> "What did I miss."

[[Calimastia]] made a deal with [[Barry]]. She'd be the [[Hound of the Sheriff]], and he'd take on the official responsibility. She didn't like being in the court. She liked being out in the field. It was a win-win.

> "But more importantly. The Prince likes you. He *hates* me. I like doing the work of Sheriff, I don't like being in the room when his grace barks his orders. I like making people fear a title. I don't like being at the beck and call of whoever has a problem with their fledgling this week. You keep your title, the Prince gets his favorite back, I get to do the work I want to do. Neither of us get bossed around all that much. Everyone wins."

[[Barry]] was a simple man. He agreed.

They went to [[Mark|Prince Sterner]] to finalize the agreement. [[Mark]] was rather upset that the new Elysium was a [[Lasombra]] stronghold, but at least his [[Sheriff]], [[Barry]] was back. They just needed to test his blood first. A formality really, just to confirm he wasn't blood bonded. For whatever reason, though, he seemed rather cagey about this.

After [[Mark]] commanded [[Barry]] to allow [[Calimastia]] to perform the test, she learned the following information:

> *[[Calimastia]] detects that [[Barry|Barry Brickhouse]] is a 10th generation Kindred, has been fed quite heavily, has not committed diablerie, and has been bonded as well as influenced by some high level Dominate skills beyond [[Mark]]'s most recent command.*

This was a difficult situation. [[Mark]] decided that they would obey the agreement to make [[The Cathedral of Blessed Sacrament|The Cathedral]] a second Elysia, but [[Calimastia]] would be directly responsible for ensuring its safety. And should something like this happen again, under her nose, she'd owe much more than a minor boon.

In the meantime, [[Barry]] would return to his post as normal, such that the [[Anarchs]] wouldn't suspect their plan had been thwarted.
