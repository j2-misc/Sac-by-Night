[[Thalia|Thalia Kairos]] and [[Frances|Father Frances]] ventured to the outskirts of town, to a condemned laboratory, where [[Oswald Foxglove|Oswald Foxglove]] made his poisons. Per [[Ryu]]'s warnings[^1], their mark would be on the lower floors, and that there would be traps along the way. They made their way down and, Obfuscated, [[Thalia]] confronted him.

> *The facility that [[Thalia]] opens up smells fragrant and floral, overwhelmingly so. The interior has been completely transformed into an interior garden, with harsh LED lights attached to the ceiling. Botanist's tools and other gardening implements are organized neatly along shelves and holders.*
> *Inside the facility is, as [[Ryu]] suggested, not something that looks like a man. The creature's torso is completely hollow, with some sort of unidentifiable plants and fungi cultivated inside. Like a terrarium. Smaller terrariums can be seen along the creature's legs and arms. Only the creature's head resembles anything humanlike.*
> *[[Oswald Foxglove|Oswald]]'s face is almost as disturbing as his body. Inverted nose, spiny, dirt covered cheeks, oversized glossy eyes. As the shadows start to creep into the room, [[Oswald Foxglove|Oswald]] looks up from his work. A torpored Kindred, organs still intact, dissected and splayed across a lab table. In a sharp, irate voice, he asks:*
> "Please, kindly, visit another time."
