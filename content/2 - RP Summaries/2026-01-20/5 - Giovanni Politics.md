> *Fiora rubs her temples.*

> "I will explain. I have decided, yes, that I am going to become the Anziani. This will not be a popular decision. Especially not once the Giovanni family sends one of their own, here."

> *Fiora refrains from delivering an insult. The man who greeted Kaveh at the door kindly offers a mug of tea. Fiora explains:*

She was assured they had almost no chance of winning, but she'd like to make sure of it. If he could make her a certain charm, it might help.
