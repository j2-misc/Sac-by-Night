[[Kingsnake]] sat down to dinner with [[Neres]] who started the conversation with a bang:

> "I am troubled. Let me not burden you with my troubles."
> *[[Neres]] insists, before abruptly asking.*
> "Tell me of your new mate."

> "Ah." *[[Kingsnake|Phoebe]] was not expecting her to be so blunt. What a strange way of wording that. She's lost for words for a few seconds.* "I was a little hesitant, considering her self image as a vigilante. And how often she throws herself into danger. And the blood bond, though she insisted her feelings were genuine. At first I thought I was just worried for the movement, considering how... short-sighted their current leaders are. Without Thalia they're just going to burn everything to the ground, and I'm sure the [[Sabbat]] or the [[Second Inquisition]] would be more than happy to sweep everything up afterwards. Then I realized maybe it wasn't just that, maybe it it was because I kinda liked her? She's charismatic, driven... and she was interested in me. She doesn't see me being a [[Setite]] as a bad thing, hell, she even sees it as a benefit, as exciting. Maybe it's foolish, but I considered every aspect, and decided to go with it." *She awaits her judgement, subtly adjusting her silverware on the table. She adds the next part more quietly, a little ashamed to say it, but hoping it might sway [[Neres]] a little more.* "Plus, I mean I'm not really all that power hungry but... if she does become the [[Baron|Baroness]]... that might have perks...?"

> *[[Neres]] stares at [[Kingsnake]] intensely with her serpentine eyes. It's hard to tell what she's thinking.*
> "You have chosen wisely. She is a fine choice of mate, should she live to see the end of the war."

[[Neres]] approved, but [[Kingsnake]] was quick to remind her that she hadn't forgotten her dedication to [[Setite|Set]]. [[Neres]] waited for [[Kingsnake]] to eat first[^1], and when confronted about it, she said her conviction had lapsed. Then, when [[Mazhar]] was brought up, [[Neres]] said she'd been planning what to do for a few days. Running wasn't an option, so they'd start by lying low.
