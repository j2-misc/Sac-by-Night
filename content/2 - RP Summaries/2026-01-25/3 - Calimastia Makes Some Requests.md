*2026-03-25 14:01 - 21:51 PST*

[[Calimastia]] met with [[Leon|Leon the Alabaster]]. They spoke in coded language about the [[Jacobi|Archmage]] and his obsessions. She requested a ritual, The Pavis of the Foul Presence. He said it didn't exist, and that she'd have to wait for something later. When she returned to her room, she found a scroll, the ritual she'd asked for.

Then, she spoke with the [[Seneschal]], [[Amy|Amy Yu]] to inquire about anything she could find on a Masquerade violator[^1].

Then, she got a text from [[Barry]].

> *[[Calimastia]] suddenly gets a text from [[Barry]].*
