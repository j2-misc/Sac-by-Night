# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

An emissary of the [[Banu Haqim]] from Persia, Kabir is the clan representative on the [[Primogen|Primogen Council]]. A dignified warrior who strictly abides by the Laws of Haqim, Kabir has mobilized the disparate warriors from each [[Camarilla]] clan into a formidiable fighting force. In doing so, he aims to demonstrate the usefulness of his clan to the [[Camarilla]], earning the [[Banu Haqim]] more power in Sacramento and greater influence in the sect's politics. However, after surviving a recent [[Sabbat]] assassination attempt, Kabir has not fully recovered, his injury seemingly beyond that of a Kindred's innate ability to heal.
# Recent History
At present, [[Kabir]]'s state has been largely ignored by the [[Primogen|Primogen Council]][^1] as well as the [[Prince]], despite the rapid reshaping of the [[Anarchs]] into a more organized group of warriors[^2].
