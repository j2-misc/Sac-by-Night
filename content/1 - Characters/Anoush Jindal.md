# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

A traveling playboy, represents the [[Ravnos]] in Sacramento. In the mortal world, Anoush is famous for his ties to the wealthy Jindal family in India and presents himself as a self-help guru. In the Kindred world, Anoush speaks eloquently and passionately about taming the Beast within, claiming to offer means of obtaining internal peace and tranquility. With his fame and family ties comes money, which he generously supplies to fund the Anarch war effort. The Camarilla suspect that the Brujah hold a debt over Anoush, and should it be paid he would cease his support for the war.
# Recent History
Anoush asked [[Thalia]] and [[Kaveh]] to look into the disappearance of [[Sabirah]] after the New Year's Attacks[^1].
