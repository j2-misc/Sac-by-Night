# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

An explosive wildcard, operates independently of [[Anoush Jindal|Anoush]] but serves the [[Anarchs]]' interests in Sacramento. In the wake of the Week of Nightmares, an event where the Ravnos antediluvian ravaged the world until being subdued, the [[Camarilla]] superstitiously hunted down members of the Ravnos Clan. In Sacramento, Sabirah seeks to take her revenge against the organization that murdered many of her friends. Sabirah browbeats and harasses the [[Camarilla]] with potentially Masquerade-breaching pranks and exposés, determined to continue unless met with proper recompense.
# Recent History
[[Sabirah]] was captured by [[Luke Grayson]] during the [[Camarilla]] attacks on New Year's[^1].
