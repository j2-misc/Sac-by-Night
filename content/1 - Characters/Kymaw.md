A [[gangrel]] serial killer from the [[Sabbat]] pack known as the "[[Rangers]]" [[Kymaw]] brutally murdered five ghouls to attract attention from the warring sects. Her goal was to find worthy challengers for her pack, lead by an "[[Inquisitor Ariel Angel|Inquisitor Angel]]", and found it in the form of [[Gabriela]] and [[Kiryu]], who knocked her to torpor in a hard fought battle. Kymaw was sacrificed to the [[The Saint|Saint]] and allowed to burn in the light of the sun.
