# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

A freelance programmer, is the [[Seneschal]] of the [[Prince]]. Amy has an intense fixation on the supernatural and the occult, diving headfirst into the most cryptic and unsettling tomes she can find. Personally handpicked by the [[Prince]] for this position, for reasons unclear to [[Reginald]], Amy as the [[Seneschal]] handles physical and digital correspondences addressed to the [[Prince]] at lightning speeds. Supposedly, Amy was recently and sharply reprimanded by an archon visiting from out of state, for reasons the [[Camarilla]] refuse to disclose and which the [[Anarchs]] seek to uncover.
