# Description
A Native American medium for beasts and spirits alike, provides counsel to [[Javier]]. Peering through the senses of his unusual friends, Roger attempts to equip the [[Anarchs]] with information on [[Camarilla]] activity throughout the city. Paranoid and superstitious, Roger suspects that something dark and cryptic lies slumbering beneath the city. To his frustration, he has not yet been granted the permission to investigate.
