# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

A cynical [[Brujah]] who nominally associates with the [[Camarilla]], and a defector from [[Jeremy|Jeremy MacNeil]]'s coalition in Los Angeles. An activist in mortal spaces, Carrie wants little to do with Kindred politics.

A woman with deep, sunken eyes. Curly grey hair, dark freckles, and a missing pinky finger on her right hand.
# Recent History
[[Calimastia]] approached Carrie and made a deal. She would raise up a [[Brujah]] [[Calimastia]]'d captured from the [[Anarchs]], raise them up to be a good upstanding [[Camarilla]], and in exchange she'd offering a trivial boon for her time and a minor boon if it worked[^1].
