# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

A skilled portrait painter, is the [[Toreador]] representative on the [[Primogen|Primogen Council]]. Infamous for personally siring much of the clan's fledglings and neonates, Amalia views herself as a mentor figure and therefore goes above and beyond to immerse them in [[Camarilla]] politics. Her stirring words and visage of matronly benevolence are the glue that bind the [[Camarilla]] together, motivating them to stand strong in the face of [[Anarchs|Anarch]] brutality. However, this benevolence sours fast whenever Amalia is questioned about her past, or her suspicious Auspex aura.
# Recent History
Amalia has done nothing of note recently%%, save for her voicing initial support for [[Calimastia]]'s ascension to [[Sheriff]][^1]%%.
