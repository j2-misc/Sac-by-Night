# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

A prolific investor and venture capitalist, is the [[Ventrue]] [[Prince]] of Sacramento. He has established professional relationships with affluent businessmen, municipal and state politicians, and respected religious and community leaders, all who seem to owe him favors. Shrewd, manipulative, and grossly wealthy, Mark primarily bankrolls the [[Camarilla]]'s efforts to maintain power in the region, ensuring the coffers of war never run dry. However, rumors from undisclosed Kindred have suggested that Sacramento's [[Ventrue]] secretly finance the [[Anarchs]]' war effort, and that Mark plays both sides against one another.

He looks to be in his 50s. Slick greying hair, deep lines in his face, stoic resting expression. His cologne is heavy and slightly unpleasant.
