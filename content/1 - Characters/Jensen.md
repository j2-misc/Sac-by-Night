# Description
*Written by Jensen's player*
## Appearance
A youthful 20 year old man with dark wavy hair, brown eyes, and round glasses. He is 5'7 with a slim frame, and wears a cyan polo with long khaki pants. He is very well read and excels in academics. He has a very calm and friendly demeanor, and is generally a very pleasant person to talk to. As a devout follower of Christ, he holds an optimistic view of life, choosing to live with virtue and seeing the best in others. These positive qualities shine in his interactions with others, leading people to generally consider him trustworthy. He most importantly values repentance and forgiveness above all, believing that no one is beyond redemption. However, he finds these beliefs challenged as he is thrust into an occult world as a cursed monster forced to partake in unholy acts just to survive. Does God embrace such inhuman, blood sucking creatures as his children, or do they belong with the demons in the pits of hell?
## History
A 13th gen that was embraced *very* recently a couple weeks ago by [[Bernard|Father Bernard]], a priest of his church. The Priest, unable to reconcile his faith and his vampiric nature, developed a corrupted version of his faith. He sought to turn one of the most faithful members of the church to recruit into his cause and validate his beliefs, but gravely miscalculated his new kindred's willingness to participate in his schemes. Under threat from Jensen to break the masquerade, the Priest resolved to flee from his position and settle down in Sacramento. Jensen followed suit, taking a short break from school in order to pursue the evildoer and put a stop to his schemes.
