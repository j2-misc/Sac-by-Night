# Description
*Written by [[Calimastia]]'s player*

Irene Ducheski is a shorter, slightly sunken woman, who is very clearly not human anymore. A natural sense of perturbing inhumanity emanates off her, typical of those on a path alternative to humanity. She has stark white hair, that dangles long, that's either a magical malfunction or just a reaction to the stresses before her embrace. She has grey eyes, had a good tan before being embraced that's been swallowed by her corpselike visage, and generally wears practical, dark clothing with plenty of pockets and folds to hide implements for Thaumaturgy. Most pressingly, is a silver anathema blade, that is her most obvious and dangerous tool. She speaks with a sweet undertone, and doesn't raise her voice...unless she's about to snap.
# Recent History
A [[Ductus]] of the [[Sabbat]], Irene has harassed [[Simon]] to try and get more information about [[Calimastia]]'s regular whereabouts[^1], negotiated for her girlfriend's release[^2], and has taken a particular interest in meeting with [[Neres]], perhaps to take advantage of her shaky alliance with the [[Anarchs]][^3].
