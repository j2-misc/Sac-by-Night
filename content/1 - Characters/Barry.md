# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

A gangster from Los Angeles, ~~is~~ was the [[Sheriff]] of Sacramento. Although possibly the least intelligent Kindred alive, Barry's usefulness comes from his seeming inability to die. As the [[Prince]]'s [[Sheriff]], Barry enforces [[Mark]]'s decrees, more often physically than verbally, and serves as a personal bodyguard to [[Mark]] and any high-ranking [[Camarilla]] in need of protection. The [[Anarchs]] suspect that Barry suffers from a potent folkloric weakness, one that might incapacitate the [[Sheriff]] and turn the tide of the war.
# Recent History
Barry was recently incapacitated and captured by one [[Thalia|Thalia Kairos]] while defending the [[Sacramento History Museum]][^1]. Since then, his whereabouts remain unknown.
