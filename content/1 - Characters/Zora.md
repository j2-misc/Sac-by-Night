# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

A capable Quietus sorcerer, is [[Kabir]]'s second-in-command. A high achiever and a proficient multitasker, Zora has taken on more clan responsibility due to [[Kabir]]'s injury and aims for a position of greater renown within the Camarilla. She feuds constantly with [[Lauren|Lauren Harris]] of the [[Tremere]], and some suspect she covets her position as [[Keeper of Elysium]]. Tarnishing her reputation, however, is the suspicion that Zora consorts with the city's [[Sabbat]], which both the [[Camarilla]] and [[Anarchs]] are eager to deny and confirm respectively.
# Recent History
Zora did most of the heavy lifting at the recent turf war at [[Crocker Art]] and has tasked her childe, [[Gabriela]], with dealing with Sacramento's [[Tzimisce]] problem[^1].
