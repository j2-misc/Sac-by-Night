# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

A night college professor at Sacramento State, is the [[Keeper of Elysium]] for the [[Camarilla]]. Most Kindred quickly find Lauren to be verbose, pedantic, and droll, an impression Lauren doesn't seem to mind. Faithful to her duties, Lauren insists that the [[Camarilla]]'s Kindred abide by the sect's traditions and the customs of Elysium to a tee. After a violent confrontation gone south with the [[Keeper of Elysium|Keeper]], the [[Anarchs]] fear that Lauren has come into the possession of some resurfaced artifact, though its potential is yet unknown.
