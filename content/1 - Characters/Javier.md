# Description
An animal rights activist, represents the [[Gangrel]] in Sacramento. Tactical and cunning, Javier commands the [[Gangrel]] to vigilantly patrol the city's outskirts, denying outside aid to the [[Camarilla]] and keeping aid flowing to the [[Anarchs]]. Javier harbors a personal vendetta against [[Reginald]] of the [[Malkavian|Malkavians]], whose cattle farms he believes are unethical, and is quick to dismantle them. It is no secret that Javier heavily dislikes working alongside [[Anoush|Anoush]] of the [[Ravnos]], and the [[Camarilla]] hope to enflame tension between the two men.
