# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

*Olive Cline, a rarely-seen strategist for the [[Anarchs]], is [[Kiri]]'s second-in-command. Some say they're a [[Brujah]], some say they're a skilled Caitiff, some say they're something more obscure. Regardless, Olive serves as the cool-headed, logical backbone of the [[Anarchs|Anarch]]'s frontline operations, commanding and maneuvering [[Anarchs|Anarch]] warriors like a master tactician. Despite his usefulness, they have a poor reputation among other [[Anarchs]], for reasons unclear to even those who dislike Olive.*
