## History
Calimastia was once an up and coming Kindred, embraced into the prestigious clan Tremere. This was back when [[Prince]] [[Dorian]] was still walking the earth, and the clan's position in the city still had status to spare, and those around Sacramento at the time, would recall that Calimastia wasn't content to rest on her laurels. Within a year of leaving her accounting, her acumen saw her becoming a Lesser [[Harpy]], dutifully serving the office and it's ledger. However, then, a lot of terrible things happened to the Warlocks. Prince Dorian was slain, the [[Tremere]] [[Primogen]] descended into something like madness, and suddenly, an up and coming prodigy wasn't anything to smile at. Someone called in a boon from somewhere, and suddenly, Calimastia was sent on a so called "Urgent Mission", to subvert the [[Sabbat]] pack "[[The Thousand Steps]]". And then, no one saw her again. Until of course, very recently, though the scarred and burned Kindred that returned, quietly delivering an urn of ashes to her baffled [[Primogen]] is hardly the same as the once conniver. Nowadays, she seems to be resting on what laurels the defeat of her quarry have to offer. For now.
# Recent History
On several occasions, Calimastia has been accosted by a woman named [[Irene|Irene Ducheski]]. [[Irene]] and Calimastia seem to have something of a complicated relationship. An old flame working for the enemy. During the events of [[1 - Raid on Sac History Museum]], [[Irene]] negotiated for Calimastia's release from [[Anarchs]] [[Kingsnake]] and [[Kaveh]]

After the events of [[1 - Raid on Sac History Museum]], Calimastia was appointed to be the Interim [[Sheriff]] of Sacramento in [[Barry|Barry Brickhouse]]'s absence[^1]. Since then she has fulfilled her role dutifully[^2].
