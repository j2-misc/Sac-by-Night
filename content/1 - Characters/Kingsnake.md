# Description
*Written by Kingsnake's player*
## Appearance
Kingsnake is a woman of average height, pale complexion and a slightly underweight but fit build. Her hair is long, black and straight, usually parted down the middle and neck length. Her signature outfit usually consists of cargo pants, a tank top and a large puffy winter coat with a fur ruff. The coat makes her seem quite a bit more physically imposing than she really is, perhaps a point of comfort for her. There's an oddly reptilian way that she interacts with the world, varying between this lax sort of state where she's rather slow to start, and periods of constant, restless activity. Outside the company of mortals, she can occasionally be seen tasting the air with flicks of a long, forked and blackened tongue.
## History
In life, Kingsnake was a career art thief and larcenist. In a grandiose series of misfortunes, she lost her stolen riches, her contacts, and ultimately her life, and stumbling into unlife alone, confused, hungry, and left to fend for herself by a sire she never even met. There were a few messy kills and trails gone cold around the city before the newly embraced kindred stumbled into [[Neres|Neres Zauvasi]] while casing the [[Sacramento History Museum|California History Museum]]'s hall of fame, leading to her introduction to kindred society. All of this took place a paltry 4 years ago, making her one of the city’s younger vampires. Since then, she's been spotted slinking around the [[Setite]] emissary's territory, looking a little out of place visiting a museum instead of robbing one, her less than formal sense of attire and sullen attitude always a little strange when placed next to her erudite and amicable hostess. Those who have interacted with her report that she seems almost allergic to respecting her elders, but can't be pressed into saying anything even the least bit negative about [[Neres]].
# Recent History
[[Kingsnake]] has primarily been fixated on pleasing [[Neres]][^1]. Partially to this end, she has taken to pursuing anti-[[Camarilla]] activity, including breaking and entering into the [[Sacramento County Administration Building]] in order to find information on [[V&S]]'s holdings[^2].
