## History
Most young brujah use their rage and violence like a hammer, leaving broken pieces everywhere. Thalia Kairos does not. She wields her rage and violence like a scalpel, carving out the rotten pieces of the world with precision. The grandchild of [[Jeremy|Jeremy MacNeil]], she followed in his footsteps, jumping from domain to domain, joining any cause that caught her eye. Very little is known about her life before her embrace, as she tends to be a very private person. What is known is that her sire, soon after her embrace, died, leaving her in the care of her grandsire. What she is doing in Sacramento is unknown, but what is clear is that she will probably be causing problems- at least for the [[Camarilla]].
