> Written by Calimastia's player

A fearsome vampiric warrior, tall and with a scar Jane Zantosa is the second known protector of [[Holly Zantosa]]. Capable of obfuscate, Jane Zantosa attempted to intervene in the first attempt on [[Holly Zantosa|Holly]]'s life, but was delayed. In the second, she slipped into Kiryu's vehicle unnoticed, and defeated him using her mastery of physical disciplines, only stopped from destroying him by the intervention of [[Mordecai|Mordecai Hayes]], who tore off her arm.
