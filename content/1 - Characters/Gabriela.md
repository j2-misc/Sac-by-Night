# Description
## Appearance
A tall woman with strong features.

![[Gabriela.png]]
## History
Gabriela was recruited to the [[Banu Haqim]] by [[Zora|Zora Tazri]] in the early 1800s as part of a plot to assassinate a [[Tzimisce]] vampire, against whom all prior attempts had failed. The exact cause of death and Gabriela's role in it is not public knowledge. Since then she has been occasionally spotted in cities across the Americas, often collecting payment for fulfilled mercenary contracts. Now with the [[Banu Haqim]] allied with the [[Camarilla]], and her sire steeped in controversy, Gabriela seems to have taken more of an interest in politics over mercenary work.
# Recent History
Gabriela has been tasked by several people to find and deal with Sacramento's [[Tzimisce]][^1]. Since then, she has done a whole lot and nothing about it, including the unsanctioned fostering of [[Sam]] (a [[Tzimisce]] neonate) and secretly consorting directly with [[Marouth]][^2].

She was also loosely involved in the capture of [[Barry|Barry Brickhouse]][^3].
