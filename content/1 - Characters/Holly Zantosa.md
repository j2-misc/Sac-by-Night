> Written by Calimastia's player

A revenant belonging to a family of other revenants, believed to be aligned with the [[Sabbat]], Holly Zantosa has a long history of instagram party girl antics which has earned her millions of followers. Due to her affiliation with the [[Sabbat]], Holly has earned a death penalty from the [[Prince]], one neither former [[Scourge]] [[Oswald|Oswald Foxglove]] or the other neonates and ancillae of the city could fulfill. Holly was wounded by [[Kiryu]] in a recent confrontation, to an unknown result.
