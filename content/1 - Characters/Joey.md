# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

The reluctant, unorthodox [[Harpy]] of Sacramento. Joey is the hotheaded, rebellious daughter of [[Amalia]], a position with heavy responsibilities that Joey tends to ignore. Instead, Joey concentrates on her career as a drummer for _The Scorned,_ a punk band popular in the Sacramento area. However, Joey does embrace her role as a [[Harpy]] when it comes to setting stylistic trends or speaking brazenly about political dealings, appealing greatly to fledgling Kindred. Aware of the [[Camarilla]]'s distaste for the [[Harpy]], the [[Anarchs]] wonder if they can sway Joey to advocate for their cause.
# Recent History
Joey has done nothing of note recently besides annoying [[Zora]][^1].
