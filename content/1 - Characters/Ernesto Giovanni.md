# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

A real estate tycoon, represents the interests of the Giovanni not just in Sacramento, but in all of California. To the discontentment of the warring sects, Ernesto stubbornly insists upon his clan's neutrality, preferring to stay out of Kindred politics. Nevertheless, both the Camarilla and Anarchs continue to court Ernesto for favors, hoping to pull the Giovanni further towards their side. What a godsend might it be if this elder, believed to be of the lowest generation in Sacramento, joined a side...
