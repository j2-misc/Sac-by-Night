# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

Oswald Foxglove, a macabe and ill-mannered botanist, is the [[Scourge]] of Sacramento. Personally tasked by [[The Talon]] to slay thin-bloods and caitiffs throughout the city, Oswald has devised a morbid and unconventional means of carrying out his Scourge duties. Within his own undead body, Oswald cultivates a terrarium of toxic, paralytic, and seizure-inducing plants, inexplicably potent enough to affect high generation Kindred. The [[Anarchs]] aim to uncover the location of his laboratory and slay the Kindred before he can strengthen the potency of his poisons.
# History
Per [[Ally Barnes]]'s request, and thanks to a tip from [[Ryu]], [[Thalia]] and [[Frances|Father Frances]] infiltrated his hideout and apprehended him while he was experimenting on [[Roy]] to try and recreate his internal terrarium. Sources have informed me he was later executed by [[Thalia|Thalia Kairos]] at the behest of [[Kiri|Kiri Sol]].
