One of the thirteen vampire clans. Uniquely, they embrace almost exclusively from within their family[^1].

They are presently unaligned with any of the three sects ([[Anarchs]], [[Camarilla]], [[Sabbat]]), though recent action by members of the [[Camarilla]] may soon change that[^2].
