## Appearance

## History
\[Redacted] was always a man of mindfulness and suspecting anyone surrounding him in his life. He could devise their implications and their goals just by their mannerisms. That's why he was always one step ahead of them, he felt comfortable being alone since nobody could even fathom his thought process on the back of his mind, albeit portraying himself as a thoughtful and kind person. He always wanted to reach to the truth of everything even if it costed an absurd amount of sacrifices, it was the only thing that could satiate him from his ever growing curiosity of the unknown.

Even the pitch black darkness at night wouldn't scare him, that was one of the reasons he even lived in such a place, the world of darkness made him find a purpose in life, to uncover something that no one has ever found before. The best way to do this was obviously to be one with the shadow and since he couldn't do that in his humane form, he reached upon a conclusion, to let himself be taken care of from the aloof feeling of someone watching him that he always had at night. Yes, he would gladly throw his life away on a simple yet unpractical thing as a gamble. One thing he didn't know unfortunately is that he was inside a haven of the [[Tzimisce]], in a place that rarely saw any visitors.

One night, feeling the same eerie presence that he always felt he went to "relax" a little at a pub. After a few shots, he got drunk and a young woman, seeing that he's pale as a ghost, decided to sit next to him. She felt worried about him and decided to escort him home. What he didn't know though is that her face was actually rearranged and since he was flat out drunk he thought his mind was playing tricks on him. It was one of the [[Tzimisce]] that has targeted the man, whilst also still feeling that something was still hiding in the shadows, There was something else observing them. When they returned to his home the man after a long while of being alone decided to invite the [[Tzimisce]] in his home, but the woman replied that this wasn't his home to begin with. It's her home because it's her domain. Then the unexpected happened. As the female [[Tzimisce]] tried to embrace him and make him one of their own, apparently, the [[Nosferatu]] were keeping a watch on them and decided to step in although a bit late. The [[Tzimisce]] bit him but not enough to complete the embrace and was assassinated by the [[Nosferatu]]. Seems like they were there for a reconnaissance mission directly assigned from the [[Camarilla]]. He was embraced by the [[Nosferatu]] but some of the previous [[Tzimisce]] influence has already resided in his body, trying to claim control over him, but the [[Nosferatu]] blood prevailed.

When he woke up he didn't know what happened but he certainly felt like his plan of leaving his humanity has worked flawlessly. While feeling like the shadow, the only downside he had was that he was plain ugly and was abhorred by the other vampires quite a bit since he was of mixed blood. This however was the best possible outcome for his goal in life, to be able to uncover the hidden truth with absolute secrecy.

Having been taught the ways of the [[Nosferatu]] by his well-respected sire, having a great deal of connections that he can abuse and a special ability that just randomly manifested when he wanted to take the role of a different persona, he is now ready
to unlive as an undead... going by the codename Kurokage.
