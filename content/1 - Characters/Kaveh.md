# Description
*Written by Kaveh's player*
## Appearance
WHEN HE IS INSIDE [[Falcron Blacksmith]]
A muscular Hispanic-Iranian man in his mid thirties (35), standing at 5'11", with a bald, polished head that catches the glow of the forge light. A black headband wraps firmly around his forehead, keeping “sweat” from his eyes. His face is rugged and imposing almost ogre-like in structure with a heavy brow, sharp cheekbones, and a long, extended goatee that frames his jaw. His eyes burn a faint, unnatural orange, somewhat mimicking the fire of the forge. He wears a fitted white long-sleeve shirt with the sleeves rolled to his forearms, revealing powerful, work-hardened arms marked by heat and steel. A worn white towel rests across his neck, and over his clothes he wears a thick black blacksmith’s apron, heavy leather adorned with hanging tools, hooks, and subtle scorch marks. 

AT NIGHT/Not in [[Falcron Blacksmith]] 
The same muscular Hispanic-Iranian man stands at 5'11", his presence sharper, colder. A black durag covers his head, secured by a thick, almost fur-textured black headband wrapped around his forehead. His orange eyes glow a little as the street lights make contact with him(he does not actually have glowing eyes) He wears a long black trench coat/coat with pronounced cuffs, draped over his shoulders rather than fully worn in a deliberate, imposing style. The coat was crafted by both him and his sire, stitched with intention and legacy. Pinned to the chest is a custom orange emblem: the Falcron logo, a falcon gripping a hammer. Beneath the coat, he wears a loose-fitting black fisherman’s sweater that softens his silhouette while hiding his strength. His black professional trousers are tailored for comfort and flexibility, allowing movement despite their refined appearance. A heavy belt wraps his waist, bearing another Falcron emblem, this one depicting a falcon holding a gavel instead of a hammer, symbolizing judgment instead of craftsmanship
