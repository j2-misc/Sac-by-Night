A grandchilde of [[Cappadocius]]. He used to have a friendly relationship with [[Neres|Neres Zauvasi]] which was ruined after [[Augustus Giovanni]] killed his wife [[Ravza]] and [[Neres]] failed to intervene somehow[^1]. Until recently, he was effectively enslaved by Clan [[Giovanni]]. Since then, he's killed [[Ernesto Giovanni]] and presumably [[Augustus Giovanni|Augustus]] as well.
