> Written by Calimastia's player

A [[Banu Haqim]] sorceress antitribu, Razdan called the [[Banu Haqim]] of Sacramento to a secret moot with ancient encoded glyphs of the clan. The [[Ductus]] of the Pack known as the "[[Acolytes of Steel and Iron]]", Razdan seems to follow a fusion of [[Sabbat]] ritae and [[Banu Haqim]] law, and assisted [[Gabriela]] and [[Kaveh]] with defeating agents of [[Ur-Shulgi]], closing a simulacrum gate. Currently stewarding a torpid and powerful sorcerer, with the promise of turning him over to the [[Camarilla]] once awoken[^1].
