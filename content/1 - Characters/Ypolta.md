A Nosferatu from the Rangers, and spotter for Kymaw, Ypolta left many of the calling cards for Kymaw's killings, and used a variety of bluffs and subterfuges to attempt to secure their escape. Gabriela's paranoia was ultimately her undoing, however, and Ypolta was sacrificed to the Saint and allowed to burn in the light of the sun.
