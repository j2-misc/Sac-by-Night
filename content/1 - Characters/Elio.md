> Description written by Calimastia's player

A powerful magician of unknown clan, Elio is the master of the strange creatures that attacked [[Mercury Labs|Mercury Laboratories]], and allowed his theft of Alchemical Formulae dear to the [[Thin Blood Coalition|Coalition]]. Tracked down by a motley of [[Anarchs]], he professed his plans to use an alchemical formula called "[[Saturn's Flux]]" to shatter all blood bonds in the city through a water supply poison. The [[Anarchs]] left him in peace, along with two defector thin bloods, in exchange for copies of the stolen formula.
