# Description
A historian at the [[Sacramento History Museum|California History musem]], represents the [[Setite|Setites]] in Sacramento, though seems to be the lone member. Enigmatic yet well-connected, Neres prefers to support the [[Anarchs]] from a distance, trading in information regarding [[Camarilla]] gathering places, persons of interest, and other cryptic lore of dubious origins. She takes a great interest in the rumors of unearthed relics throughout Sacramento, employing Kindred who would retrieve them for her. In her pursuit of lost relics, Neres has frequently clashed with the [[Tremere]], and her presence strikes inexplicable fright within [[Jacobi|Archmage Jacobi]].
