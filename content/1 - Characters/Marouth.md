# Description
*Description by the Storyteller*

Exceptionally tall, lithe, and dressed in the same gothic-era gown as [[Gabriela]]'s first Kindred kill. Stout ebony horns grow from her temples, her cheekbones are sharp angles, and her nails are daggers.
# Recent History
[[Marouth]] returned to Sacramento after a forty year absence[^1] to find it a warzone between the [[Camarilla]] and the [[Anarchs]]. Displeased by the gang war over her city, it seems she's formed an alliance with the [[Sabbat]] and begun gathering power while residing in [[Sutter Park|Sutter's Fort]][^2]. This has put her at odds with her old flame, [[Gabriela]], a fact both seem to have rather complicated feelings about.
