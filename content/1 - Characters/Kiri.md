# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

A vocal organizer championing the Free States, is the aspiring [[Baron]] of Sacramento. Loud and brash, Kiri commits the [[Anarchs]] to all-out war against the [[Camarilla]], believing them to be a vile rot that must be purged. Rather than conducting warfare from a distance, Kiri fights directly on the front lines and is personally responsible for delivering Final Death to over a dozen Kindred just in the past few months. However, the [[Anarchs]] worry that Kiri's extremism are frightening neutral parties into joining up with the [[Camarilla]], a result the [[Camarilla]] are all too happy to see.
