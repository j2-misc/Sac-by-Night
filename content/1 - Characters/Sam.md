# Description
# Recent History
A vagrant originally from Chicago, Sam traveled to California for the more hospitable winter weather. She was embraced by [[Marouth]] sometime in early or mid December[^1]. A botched hunt of hers drew the attention of [[Mordecai|Lieutenant Hayes]] who then tasked [[Gabriela]] with looking into it[^2]. There Sam confused [[Gabriela]] for a police officer or hunter and attacked her. This didn't go well for her, and she was then questioned about her sire. Eventually, [[Marouth]] appeared and, in her frustration, nearly killed Sam but was stopped by [[Gabriela]].

Since then she's stayed with [[Gabriela]] who, as it turns out, is not a particularly good mentor[^3]. Her whereabouts are presently unknown[^4].
