# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

A reclusive archivist, is the [[Tremere]] representative on the [[Primogen|Primogen Council]]. Archaic and obsessed with the past, Jacobi sequesters himself in his archives, devising new forms of blood sorcery and ruminating upon cryptic, ancient lore. Although bitter, difficult, and arguably as mad as a [[Malkavian]], the [[Camarilla]] relies upon his wealth of knowledge, especially to gain the upper hand against the [[Anarchs]]. Jacobi's latest fascination centers around a buried key, one he believes will lead to and unlock a long-forgotten vault beneath the city.
