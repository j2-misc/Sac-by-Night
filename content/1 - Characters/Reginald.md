# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

A self-proclaimed humanitarian, is the [[Malkavian]] representative on the [[Primogen|Primogen Council]]. Seeking to make feeding safer and more humane in Sacramento, Reginald has created a sophisticated network of blood donation facilities and livestock farms to feed the [[Camarilla]]. However, the scale of Reginald's grandiose projects exceeds what the [[Camarilla]] are willing to fund in this time of war, spurring the desperate visionary to search for a miracle. Reginald's search has taken him to many dangerous and highly contested regions of the city, exposing him to the front lines of the war. Despite this, the ambitious, optimistic [[Malkavian]] firmly believes his miracle will soon manifest.
