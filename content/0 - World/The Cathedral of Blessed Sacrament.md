# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

The Cathedral of the Blessed Sacrament is a historical place of worship for the Roman Catholic Church, dates back to 1889. This cathedral continues to serve Sacramento's Catholic community and offers Mass in multiple languages. A pair of visiting priests from different parts of the United States have recently arrived to stay at the Cathedral, one [[Bernard|Father Bernard]] and one [[Lorenzo|Father Lorenzo]]. They are eager to minister to the congregation.
