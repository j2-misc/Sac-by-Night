One of the thirteen vampire clans. Uniquely, they embrace almost exclusively from within their family[^1].

They are presently unaligned with any of the three sects ([[Anarchs]], [[Camarilla]], [[Sabbat]]), though recent action by members of the [[Camarilla]] may soon change that[^2].
# Families
Clan Giovanni is made up of several families with significant holdings in specific regions, including the Pisanobs of Mexico and South America, the Milliners of the United States, the Dunsirns of Scotland and the UK, and of course the Giovanni of Italy. The Giovanni, of course, hold the most power within the clan. Most [[Anziani]] are Giovanni, as a result[^3].

Notably, the Milliners are the most recent addition to the clan, only joining around the 1950s.
