# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

A quaint, Victorian-style hotel in Sacramento's Downtown district serves as the primary site of Elysium for the city's high-profile [[Camarilla]]. Behind the closed doors of conference rooms, [[Camarilla|The Ivory Tower]] frequently convenes here to discuss matters of key importance. The hotel is a hub of activity for those seeking a higher status in the [[Camarilla]] hierarchy, granting opportunities to meet your fellow [[Camarilla]], stay up-to-date about the goings-on of Kindred society, and even speak to the clan leaders themselves.

# Recent History
As it is the primary site of Elysium, it was the site where [[Calimastia]] ascended to the position of [[Sheriff]][^1] and [[Simon]] surrendered the artifacts stolen from [[Neres]]'s [[Sacramento History Museum]][^2].
