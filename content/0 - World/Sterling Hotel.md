# Description
*Written by the Storyteller*

A quaint, Victorian-style hotel in Sacramento's Downtown district serves as the primary site of Elysium for the city's high-profile Camarilla. Behind the closed doors of conference rooms, the Ivory Tower frequently convenes here to discuss matters of key importance. The hotel is a hub of activity for those seeking a higher status in the Camarilla hierarchy, granting opportunities to meet your fellow Camarilla, stay up-to-date about the goings-on of Kindred society, and even speak to the clan leaders themselves.
