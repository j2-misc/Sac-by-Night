One of the thirteen vampire clans[^1]. Presently in Sacramento, the Banu Haqim are represented on the [[Primogen|Primogen Council]] by [[Kabir|Kabir Rafati]]. %%Represented is used usely here, though as [[Kabir]]'s absence from the [[Primogen|Council]] was left unmentioned at [[Calimastia]]'s ascent to [[Sheriff]][^2].%%

As the Banu Haqim's alliance with the [[Camarilla]] is rather recent, it seems at times to some members of the clan that they receive far less respect than they deserve, given their contributions[^3]. [[Zora|Zora Tazri]], for one, has taken on many responsibilities especially in the wake of [[Kabir]]'s failed assassination, though this has done nothing but spawn rumors she covets the position of the [[Keeper of Elysium]] or that she consorts with the [[Sabbat]].
