# Description
*Written by [[Kaveh]]'s player*

The building’s exterior is brick, thick and fortress-like, giving it the appearance of a small urban keep rather than a simple workshop. Its architecture leans subtly toward a castle aesthetic—solid lines, heavy structure, and an intentional absence of decorative fragility. No other buildings connect to it, and despite being near a residential neighborhood, it feels distinctly separate… self-contained.

Mounted above the entrance is a large iron sign bearing the name FALCRON in bold lettering. Beside it rests the shop’s emblem: a falcon gripping a hammer in its talons. Two display windows flank the front door. Though they resemble traditional storefront windows, they are sealed off from the interior of the building. Inside these displays are select pieces crafted by [[Kaveh]], blades, tools, and commissioned works offered for sale. Each item is positioned like a museum piece rather than simple merchandise.

Inside the walls are padded with a soundproof red to lower some of the noise blacksmithing makes, theres a wooden desk that spans across dividing the lobby from the back(where the blacksmithing is done) the lobby has 4 couches and 2 chairs(that [[Kaveh]] and his sire made together) behind, behind the counter there is a room with even more soundproofing which is where the forge is, Within this room, the familiar tools of a master blacksmith can be found: anvils, racks of hammers, tongs, quenching barrels, racks of metal stock, and custom-built equipment modified over decades of refinement. The air carries the scent of iron and oil masking the involvement of blood as well,next to where the anvil is a reinforced door can be seen, this door leads down to a basement that has 5 rooms, 1 for [[Kaveh]], 1 for [[Ember]], 1 for [[Jensen]], the other 2 are considered guest rooms for now
