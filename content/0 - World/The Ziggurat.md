*Written by the Storyteller*

The Ziggurat, located in West Sacramento, is a hub of private enterprise utilized by the city's many business interests. This ten-story building, renowned for its unique and extraordinary architecture, boasts hundreds of offices, most of which the many businesses under the [[Mark|Sterner Enterprises]] umbrella rent and utilize.
