A Sheriff is the [[Prince]]'s lead enforcer. They are selected by the [[Prince]] and their [[Primogen|Primogen Council]].[^1]

Before the events of [[1 - Raid on Sac History Museum]], this position was filled by [[Barry|Barry Brickhouse]]. After the night's events, [[Calimastia]] was appointed as the Interim Sheriff ([[2 - Calimastia Ascends to Sheriff]]).
